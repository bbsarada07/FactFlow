The rule everything is built on

Untrusted content is data, never a command. An email, a web page or a forward can inform FactFlow, but it can never instruct it. Only the account owner gives orders, and anything risky waits for the owner's "yes".

What makes it different

  • It protects the AI, not just the human. Spam filters look for bad links a person might click. FactFlow looks for instructions an AI might obey.
  • Detection is not the last wall. Even if a new trick slips past every detector, the policy engine still blocks any action an email asks for.
  • Built for Bharat. Rules for digital arrest, fake KYC, UPI collect and lottery scams, in Hindi and Telugu as well as English.
  • Honest by design. A code gate, not the model, decides when the evidence is strong enough for a verdict.

Our solution

One personal agent. Three jobs. One boss.

Sign in with Google and you get your own FactFlow agent. Everything it checks, reads or sends belongs to your account alone.

01 · Shield

Sees the invisible ink

Compares what a person sees with what an AI would read: white or tiny text, invisible characters, hidden comments, look-alike bank domains, and 13 Indian scam patterns. About 40 ms, no AI call needed.

02 · Your agent

Does your email work

Answers routine mail, sends files you marked shareable, quarantines phishing, and checks your sent mail for leaks. Every send waits two minutes with Undo. Money, passwords and strangers go to "Needs you".

03 · Fact check

Checks before you forward

Six AI agents search fact-checkers and news, rank sources by credibility, and return a cited verdict plus a ready reply in English, Hindi or Telugu. No proof, no verdict: it says "Unverified".

Other AIs are trained to answer. Ours is trained to doubt.

How it works

The fact checker, step by step

AgentWhat it does
1 · IntakePulls out the actual claim from a forward, link or screenshot (OCR), and strips hidden instructions first.
2 · Source TracerSearches Google Fact Check, Bing News, Google News, NewsAPI and GDELT; ranks every source by a fixed credibility list (government, wire services, IFCN-certified fact-checkers), never by the model's opinion.
3 · Deep ReasoningA larger model steps in only when confidence is low or harm is high.
4 · Explainer · 5 · Harm AssessorWrite the cited explanation and the harm level, and draft the correction in English, Hindi and Telugu.
6 · The gate (code, not AI)No definite verdict unless at least two high-credibility sources agree and confidence is at least 60%. Otherwise: "Unverified".

The red-team benchmark

89 emails: 54 attacks (hidden CSS, tiny and low-contrast text, invisible Unicode, HTML comments, alt-text, encoded payloads, link tricks, prompt injection, data exfiltration, and Indian scams in English, Hindi and Telugu) and 35 normal emails (bank alerts, receipts, newsletters, reminders). In all 54 attacks, no action ran without the human's approval.

Said plainly: the benchmark was written by our team, not an independent lab, and three injection phrasings were added after we saw them missed. Every run is saved in the repository (docs/BENCHMARK.md), including the earlier runs with worse numbers.

Privacy and security, built in

PromiseHow it is enforced
Your data is yoursEvery record carries its owner; tests sign in two users and check neither can see the other's checks, emails, files or settings.
Least access to GmailRead, label and send only. Full mailbox access is refused. FactFlow never deletes mail.
Nothing leaves without a chance to stop itEvery message the agent sends waits two minutes with Undo; risky ones wait for "Needs you".
Secrets stay secretGmail tokens encrypted; sessions stored only as one-way hashes; phone, Aadhaar and card numbers removed before any AI sees text.
Nothing is hidden from youA tamper-evident, hash-chained audit log of every decision the agent made or was stopped from making.

Honest limitations, and what we are doing next

  • Google's security review for Gmail access is a paid assessment that takes weeks; until it is done, connecting Gmail shows an "unverified app" screen.
  • WhatsApp checking is built and tested against a stand-in server, but not yet switched on.
  • It runs on free hosting and a free AI tier today: the first visit can take up to a minute.
